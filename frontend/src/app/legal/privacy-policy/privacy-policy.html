Privacy Policy

 Last updated: 12 October 2026

This site is a student project built for the 42 curriculum (ft_transcendence) by sbruma, pamatya, mkijewsk and maba: an online arcade where you play rock-paper-scissors and tic-tac-toe against other players. Contact: transcendence.42.eval@gmail.com.

What we store

  • Account: username, email address and a hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • Avatar: the image you upload, or the default one.
  • Games: your finished matches (game, result, score, opponent, date), and the XP, level, achievements and settings that come from them.
  • Friends and chat: your friend requests and the messages you exchange with friends.
  • API keys: if you create one, only a hash of it.

We don't collect anything else and we don't use advertising or tracking.

Cookies

One cookie, only to keep you logged in. It is deleted when you log out.

Why

Only to run the site: logging in, playing matches, showing your history, stats, achievements and the leaderboard, and letting you add friends and chat. We never sell or share your data.

Who can see what

Other logged-in players can see your username, avatar, level, achievements and match history. Only your friends see whether you're online. Only you and the other person can read a chat. Nobody else sees your email or password.

How long

As long as the project runs. The database may be wiped at any time, for example after the evaluation.

Your rights

You can change your username and avatar in your profile. To see, correct or delete your data, write to transcendence.42.eval@gmail.com.
